Interpretation limits
=====================

The initial mount and structural-removal rows still perform full reconciliation
and include Store scheduling. Matching and unrelated rows reuse an unchanged
lineage/revision stamp and therefore avoid entry scans. Local medians are
whole-runtime observations. They do not establish cross-host or release gates.
